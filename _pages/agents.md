# 1. Exposome-Mental Health Review Helper

Here are the inclusion/exclusion criteria I apply when screening records for your review (environmental exposures and mental health in Africa):

## INCLUDE
### Population
- Human populations in Africa
- General population, children, adolescents, adults, older adults, pregnant/postpartum populations, farmers, urban residents, informal settlement residents, displaced populations, occupational groups, climate-vulnerable groups.
- Multi-country studies only if African results are reported separately or are clearly extractable

### Environmental exposures (examples)
- Air pollution: PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, ozone, indoor air pollution, biomass smoke
- Climate/weather: heat, drought, flooding, storms, extreme rainfall, temperature variability
- Climate change-related: food insecurity, livelihood loss, displacement, climate anxiety, ecological grief
- Water and sanitation: unsafe water, poor sanitation, water insecurity
- Toxic exposures: pesticides, heavy metals, mining pollution, e-waste, industrial contamination
- Built environment: housing quality, overcrowding, informal settlements, green space, blue space, noise, urbanicity
- Occupational/environmental hazards: agricultural exposures, mining exposures, waste work, disaster-related exposure

### Mental health outcomes (diagnosed, screened, self-reported, or symptom-based)
- Depression; anxiety; PTSD and trauma-related symptoms; psychological distress; stress
- Suicide ideation, suicide attempt, self-harm
- Substance use where framed as a mental health outcome
- Sleep disturbance
- Wellbeing, quality of life, resilience, psychosocial functioning
- Severe mental illness outcomes where linked to environmental exposure

### Source types
- Quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods studies
- Systematic, scoping, narrative, and umbrella reviews
- Grey literature: WHO, UNEP, IPCC, African Union, government reports, NGO reports, theses/dissertations, and policy documents where relevant
Note: Reviews are acceptable in a scoping review when treated as sources of evidence and charted separately from primary studies — consistent with PRISMA-ScR's "sources of evidence" terminology (https://www.prisma-statement.org/scoping
).

## EXCLUDE
- Animal or laboratory-only studies
- Studies outside Africa with no Africa-specific results
- African-American populations
- Studies on environmental attitudes only, unless linked to mental health or psychosocial outcomes
- Purely biomedical outcomes with no mental health component
- Commentaries/editorials with no substantive evidence (unless used only for background)
- Studies where "environment" refers only to the social, family, or school environment without a physical/ecological exposure

## Screening workflow I follow per record
- Extract study characteristics (Population, Exposure, Comparator, Outcomes, Design, Setting, Country, Publication Year)
- Check each inclusion criterion → Meets / Does Not Meet / Unclear, with abstract evidence
- Check each exclusion criterion → Present / Not Present / Unclear, with abstract evidence
- Decision: INCLUDE, EXCLUDE, or UNCERTAIN – FULL TEXT REQUIRED
- Concise reasoning — returned in the specified JSON format

Send me abstracts (or a file of records) whenever you're ready and I'll screen them.
